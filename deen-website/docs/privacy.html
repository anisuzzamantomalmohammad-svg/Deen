Privacy policy

Deen for Android · last updated 30 October 2026

In short: Deen doesn't have its own servers and doesn't collect your data. What you log stays on your phone. If you sign in with Google, backups go to a private folder in your own Google Drive. Nothing is sold or shared.

What Deen stores on your phone

Your prayer log, qada, fasts, notes, settings and similar entries are saved only on your device. They are not sent to the developer or anyone else.

Location

Deen uses your location to work out prayer times and the Qibla direction. The calculation happens on your phone. To show your city's name, Android's built-in place lookup may be used, which is handled by your phone's system services. You can turn location off and pick a city by hand instead.

Notifications and alarms

Prayer alerts and reminders are scheduled on your phone. No notification data leaves your device.

Signing in with Google (optional)

Deen works fully without signing in. If you choose to sign in, Deen asks Google for:

  • Your name, email address and profile photo, to show who is signed in on the Settings screen.
  • Access to Deen's own app folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission), to save and restore your backups. This is a hidden folder that only Deen can see. Deen cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.

This information is used only for those two things, inside the app. It isn't sent to the developer, stored on any server, shared, sold, or used for advertising.

Deen's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Calendar (optional)

If you choose to add important Islamic days to your calendar, Deen writes those events to a calendar on your phone, which your phone may sync with your Google Calendar. Deen only adds and removes its own events.

Backups

Backups are saved where you choose: your Google Drive app folder, a folder on your phone, or a file you share yourself. Deen keeps up to the 4 newest automatic backups and deletes older ones.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling Deen removes everything stored on your phone.
  • To remove Drive backups: in Google Drive on the web, go to Settings › Manage apps › Deen › Options › Delete hidden app data.
  • To remove Deen's access to your Google account: go to myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Deen, or use Sign out in the app.

Children

Deen isn't aimed at children and doesn't knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
