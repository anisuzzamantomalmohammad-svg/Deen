Deen

Prayer times, Qibla, Hijri calendar and daily worship

Deen is a free Android app for Muslims. It works out prayer times for where you are, shows the Qibla, keeps a Hijri calendar based on moon sighting, and helps you keep track of your prayers, qada and fasts. It's in English and Bangla.

  • Prayer times with alerts, and a home-screen widget with a live countdown
  • Qibla compass and distance to Makkah
  • Hijri calendar with important days, White Days and Sunnah fasts
  • Prayer and qada tracking, month reports and a year in review
  • Duas, short guides and the surahs for salah, with sources
  • Zakat, Fitrah, Fidya and Kaffarah calculator

Deen has no ads and no account of its own. Everything you log stays on your phone. If you choose to sign in with Google, your backup goes into your own Google Drive, where only Deen can read it.
