Privacy policy

Deen for Android · last updated 6 November 2026

In short: Deen doesn't have its own servers and doesn't collect your data. What you log stays on your phone. If you sign in with Google, backups and sync go to a private folder in your own Google Drive. Nothing is sold or shared.

What Deen stores on your phone

Your prayer log, qada, fasts, notes, settings and similar entries are saved only on your device. They are not sent to the developer or anyone else.

Location

Deen uses your location to work out prayer times and the Qibla direction. The calculation happens on your phone. To show your city's name, Android's built-in place lookup may be used, which is handled by your phone's system services. You can turn location off and pick a city by hand instead.

Mosques near me. Only when you open this page (or tap Refresh), Deen sends your approximate position and the search distance to the OpenStreetMap Overpass service (overpass-api.de, or overpass.kumi.systems as a backup) to find mosques around you. Nothing else is sent with it, and the result isn't saved. These services are run by third parties under their own privacy terms. If you never open this page, no location leaves your phone.

Notifications and alarms

Prayer alerts and reminders are scheduled on your phone. No notification data leaves your device.

Signing in with Google (optional)

Deen works fully without signing in. If you choose to sign in, Deen asks Google for:

  • Your name and email address (your basic Google profile), to show who is signed in. Deen shows your initials, not your photo.
  • Access to Deen's own app folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission), to save and restore your backups and to keep two phones signed in to the same account in sync. This is a hidden folder that only Deen can see. Deen cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.

This information is used only for these things, inside the app. It isn't sent to the developer, stored on any server, shared, sold, or used for advertising.

Deen's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Calendar (optional)

If you choose to add important Islamic days to your calendar, Deen writes those events to a calendar on your phone, which your phone may sync with your Google Calendar. Deen only adds and removes its own events.

Backups and sync

Backups are saved where you choose: your Google Drive app folder, a folder on your phone, or a file you share yourself. Automatic backups to Drive are made only when something has changed, and Deen keeps up to the 4 newest and deletes older ones.

If you sign in with the same Google account on two phones, Deen keeps one sync file in the same hidden app folder so both phones show the same log. Each phone's theme, language and location stay on that phone and are not backed up or synced.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling Deen removes everything stored on your phone.
  • To remove Drive backups: in Google Drive on the web, go to Settings › Manage apps › Deen › Options › Delete hidden app data.
  • To remove Deen's access to your Google account: go to myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Deen, or use Sign out in the app.

Children

Deen includes simple learning games for children (Arabic letters, duas and the steps of wudu and salah). They work offline and keep only progress and best scores, along with the rest of your log. Deen doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
